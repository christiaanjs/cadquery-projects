About

Sliding dropouts (UDH / thru-axle)

Parametric left and right sliding dropout inserts for a 12 mm thru-axle rear end:

  • Right (drive): SRAM UDH hangerless interface (coaxial hanger / Full Mount RD).
  • Left (non-drive): thru-axle boss and a choice of rear brake mount: flat (34 mm), post (74 mm), IS2000 (51 mm) or none, positioned for any rotor size.
  • Both: axle X/Y offset from the frame's nominal position, and a hub width offset to fit a wider (or narrower) hub than the frame was built for.

Status

draft. Geometry follows the specs in this folder but has not been checked against SRAM's 3D clearance models or a real caliper. Not yet made.

Shared parameters are PARAMS in models/_common.py; side-specific ones are RIGHT_PARAMS, LEFT_PARAMS and FRAME_PLATE there too. dropout_left.py / dropout_right.py merge them into their own PARAMS, and assembly.py uses all of them, so edit _common.py to change both sides together.

Construction

Modelled on the Tange sliding inserts in the pictures here (tange-*.jpg/png): a flat plate with a raised stadium key around two clamp bolts. Each insert sits on the inboard face of the frame's socket plate with its key riding in the frame slot (slot_travel of adjustment). Two M6 bolts go through an outer clamp plate and the slot into tapped holes in the key; an M4 adjuster screw is tapped into the tongue's front end. The frame plate stops ahead of the axle boss (frame_edge_x), so the boss can be as thick as the interface needs. cqp build prints the minimum frame plate rear edge for the current axle offset.

Coordinates: origin = the frame's nominal axle centre, X forward, Z up, +Y = left. The front view looks at the drive side; back at the brake side.

Interface dimensions used

UDH (drive side), from udh_frame_spec.py / udh-spec.pdf sheets 1–3: - Frame span (inboard faces) OLD + 7, so each inboard face sits at (OLD + 7)/2. - Hanger pocket 4 deep from the inboard face (floor = datum B), radius 15.5 (R15 MIN + position tol.). - 12.5 from datum B to the outboard face, so the boss is 16.5 thick. Ø20.05 bore (20 +0.1/0). - Forward rotational stop: pocket wall 14.4 from the axle, normal 30° above +X (forward-up). The build checks its contact area is ≥ 10 mm². The pocket is open behind and below the axle for the ≥ 60° backward rotation. - Boss outer radius 19 (R14.5–19.0).

Non-drive: Ø26 hub-contact protrusion 3.5 mm proud of the inboard face (sheet 1 "alternative design", Ø25 min), Ø12.2 axle hole, axle-head face at OLD/2 + D.

Brake (from disc_brake_mounts.py): face/bolt ordinates from Verdone's linear model for the chosen rotor. Lateral position: hub rotor inner face (142: 53.25, 148: 55.25) plus the mount's screw offset (post 9.55, flat 12.20, IS 15.25). Bosses are kept rotor_clearance (2 mm) outboard of the rotor. Post: M6 tapped, 13 deep. Flat: M5 clearance holes (bolts from below). IS: Ø6.2 plain holes.

Notes and caveats

  • udh_frame_spec.py labels the 12.5 mm as "recess floor to inboard face". In Section Z-Z the hanger is on the left (inboard) and the 12.5 runs from datum B to the right-hand face, so it is modelled here as datum B to the outboard face. Check this against the PDF.
  • disc_brake_mounts.py has threaded_in_frame=True for flat mount but also says the bolts go in from below the chainstay. The model uses clearance holes; change flat_hole_dia to 4.2 for M5 threads.
  • The brake mount orientation (brake_angle) is free: rotating the caliper about the axle keeps it on the rotor. Pick an angle that clears your stays; the web joining the mount to the insert is a plain convex hull in the plate plane.
  • Rotor lateral data only covers 142 and 148 mm hubs. Other hub widths raise an error unless brake_mount = "none".
  • Verdone's model diverges from Shimano's post-mount numbers at 180/203 mm (see disc_brake_mounts.compare_post_mount_to_shimano).
  • UDH use needs a (free) SRAM licence. Verify the right insert against the hangerless clearance model 90-7518-190-070 Rev H before making parts. Dropouts are safety-critical.

Materials

Intended for 7075-T6 or 6061-T6 aluminium, or stainless steel, machined. Contact faces around the axle must be flat and left unpainted.
