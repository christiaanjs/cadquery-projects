About

Recession plane check

Checks the western wall (and roof) of a building against a 45° recession plane that starts at 2.5 m above ground on the western boundary. Measurements come from a site sketch; the model reports clearance at the north eave, the ridge and the south eave, and builds the breach solid (if any) where the building pokes above the plane.
